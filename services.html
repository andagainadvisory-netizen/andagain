Advisory services suite

Ten services.
One advisory partner.

A professional advisory firm, responsible for the quality of its advice. Not a typing centre or licence reseller.

01 · Mainland & Free Zone

UAE Business Setup

Jurisdiction and structure advisory, licence category selection, trade name registration, PRO and government liaison, bank account facilitation, and visa processing for investors, partners and staff. We advise on the structure before anything is filed.

02 · Market validation

Feasibility Studies

A structured, evidence-based assessment of whether your venture is viable in the UAE. Demand, competition, regulation and cost of entry, financial reality, and risk. Three tiers, from concept validation to bank-ready analysis.

03 · Investor-ready

Business Plans

Documentation built for the people who will read it: banks, investors and licensing authorities. Market assessment, operating model, financial projections and entry route, in a format a compliance team can work with.

04 · UAE intelligence

Market Research

Category sizing, competitor and pricing maps, supplier and buyer shortlists, and regulatory position for your specific activity. Original research, not a template.

05 · Path to launch

Market Entry Strategy

A structured path from interest to operation: which market segment, which structure, which sequence, and what it costs at each step.

06 · CT & FTA

Corporate Tax & Compliance

Corporate Tax registration with the Federal Tax Authority, first-period setup, and a compliance calendar covering every date that costs money if missed. We assess the 0% Free Zone conditions properly, never assume them.

07 · VAT compliance

VAT Registration & Filing

Threshold monitoring, registration when it becomes due, and filing coordination with accounting partners, so VAT never becomes a year-end surprise.

08 · Beyond the licence

Relocation & Concierge

Life beyond the licence: housing orientation, schooling, banking for the family, and the practical landing support that makes the move real.

09 · Scaling

Business Expansion Advisory

Second licences, mainland branches, new activities and visa quota increases, planned around what the business actually needs next.

10 · Long-term partner

Ongoing Business Support

Renewals handled before they become problems, regulatory updates that affect you, new visas as the team grows, and partner introductions when you need them.

Bundled into the standard setup fee: basic medical insurance for Emirates ID and residence visa, and corporate bank account setup, from bank selection and KYC file preparation through the bank's review to account opening.
