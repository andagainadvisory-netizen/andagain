How it works

Five clear stages.
One advisory partner.

Most clients go from first conversation to a registered company in two to three weeks. No guesswork at any stage.

01

Consultation

Free, no obligation. We learn about your business, what you sell, to whom, and what matters most in the structure. In English or Amharic.

You get: a clear picture of your options

02

Structure & Pathway

Free Zone or Mainland. Which jurisdiction, which licence, what it costs. Government and third-party costs sit in one column, our fee in another. Never blended.

You get: a recommended structure with itemised costs

03

Formation

Trade name, licence application, shareholder documentation, regulatory submissions. We coordinate with a named contact inside the authority, not a generic queue. Typical timeline: 7–14 business days.

You get: your licence issued and company registered

04

Activation

Visas, Emirates ID, corporate bank account, compliance, workspace. Medical fitness test and Emirates ID are part of the deal we give you, guided end to end. A KYC file prepared the way a bank compliance team reads it.

You get: an operational company, not just a licence

05

Ongoing Support

Renewals, regulatory updates, new visas, partner introductions. A compliance calendar covering every date that costs money if missed, built at handover.

You get: deadlines handled before they become problems
