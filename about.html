Why we exist

Between the volume shops and the improvisers.

Large setup providers optimise for volume. Small agents lack structure, compliance discipline and partner reach. We sit deliberately between: a lean, partner-powered advisory that holds your relationship from decision to operation, while free zones and licensed execution partners do the processing.

Case study · Senait Global L.L.C-FZ
A tourism vision from Ethiopia became a licensed Dubai company: formed, tax-registered, and operating.

The brief

Inbound and outbound tour operations plus events, serving travellers between Ethiopia and the Gulf. The founder needed full foreign ownership, a bankable structure, and tax registration done properly from day one.

The structure

Meydan Free Zone LLC with three licensed activities: In-Bound Tour Operator, Out-Bound Tour Operator, and Events Organizing and Managing. 100% foreign owned.

Delivered

Licence and certificate of formation, MOA and share register, facility lease, Corporate Tax registration with the Federal Tax Authority with the first period set, and a renewal calendar tracked through 2027.
