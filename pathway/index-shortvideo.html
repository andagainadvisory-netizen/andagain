Free Zone or Mainland.
The wrong answer costs you for years.Free Zone ወይስ Mainland።
የተሳሳተው ምርጫ ለዓመታት ያስከፍልዎታል።

Most people setting up in the UAE start by asking what a licence costs. It is the wrong first question.

The first question is where your customers are. If they are outside the UAE, a free zone licence is usually right. If you want to sell to customers inside the UAE, a free zone licence does not allow that directly, and the quote you were sent will not mention it.

Getting it wrong is not fatal. It is expensive. You restructure a year in, pay a second set of fees, and explain the change to a bank that was already unsure about you.

And the licence is not the finish line. It is the starting line. Corporate bank accounts are where most new companies actually stall, and most of that outcome is decided on the day you choose your activity and your structure.
