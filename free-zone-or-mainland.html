Choose your structure

Free Zone or Mainland.
The first decision that matters.

It shapes your ownership, market access, cost, visas and tax. We help you decide it first, with full clarity.

Option 01

Free Zone

  • 100% foreign ownership
  • Lower setup cost, faster licensing
  • Tax incentives on qualifying income
  • Flexible visa quotas
  • Advised and coordinated directly by And Again Advisory

Best for international trade, services, and lean overhead.

Option 02

Mainland

  • Direct access to the full UAE market
  • Eligible for government contracts
  • Operate anywhere in the UAE
  • Broader activity scope
  • All seven emirates covered, priced on a clear flat-fee ladder

Best for local market focus, retail, and expansion.

What we weigh with you

Six factors decide it. We walk all six.

01

Cost

Setup and renewal, government and advisory, one-off and annual. Never blended, always itemised.

02

Visa needs

03
